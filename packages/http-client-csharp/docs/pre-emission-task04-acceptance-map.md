## Evidence columns and checkpoints

For each selected occurrence, record original fixture and unchanged expectation,
actual producer and standard writer slot, role/child path, scope and target,
source/configuration/import/trivia dependencies, existing versus missing facts,
and evidence for original reproduction, prepared decision, raw spelling and
fallback, actual pipeline, native binding/diagnostics and runtime/source text.
A retained sibling needs an independent occurrence-owned denial. A row is not
complete when only its proof producer or legacy result exists.

* **F:** real Foo local slice and admission preflight.
* **D:** common declarations, with their distinct scopes and existing field controls.
* **S:** shapes/prefixes and independently eligible children.
* **E:** ordinary type-only operands and their construction/source controls.
* **R:** remaining certified advanced roles and language gates.

The order is a working sequencing aid, not a reassignment. An unresolved
representation requiring substantial new emission infrastructure needs a scope
and cost decision; its accepted positive stays pending.
